Dobi agent

Threads assistant of Dobi — iimagazin.ru

Dobi agent manages one Threads account, @dobi_agent, on behalf of its owner. It:

  • publishes the account's own posts with practical tips on consumer rights, insurance and household questions;
  • answers comments under the account's own posts;
  • finds recent public threads where the author explicitly invites people to introduce their projects, and leaves one short introduction of Dobi (one reply per thread, at most 30 per day).

The app does not sell or share data, does not message users privately, and stores only the IDs of posts it has already answered, to avoid repeating itself.
