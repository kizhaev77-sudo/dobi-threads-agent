2. What the app does

Dobi agent is an internal tool that manages the Threads account @dobi_agent through the official Threads API. It publishes the account's own posts, replies to comments under them, and replies once in public threads where the author invites people to introduce their projects.

3. Who may use it

The app is used only by its operator for the operator's own Threads account. Anyone who connects a Threads account to the app must be at least 18 years old and must own that account.

4. Acceptable use

The app follows the Threads Terms of Use and Community Guidelines. It limits activity to one reply per thread and at most 30 replies per day, skips threads older than 24 hours, and does not reply to negative or unrelated content.
